# DCP-Project

## About the Project

Study Efficiency Lab is a Data Centric Programming project focused on tracking my personal study habits and exploring factors that may be related to study productivity.

I collect data about my study sessions, including:

When I start studying

Which subject I study

How long each session lasts

How many times I get distracted

Where I study


## Project questions

1. Do longer study sessions tend to have more distractions?
2. Does the subject I’m studying affect the length of time it takes to learn it and the number of distractions?

These questions may be changed or refined as the dataset grows.

## CSV Data Dictionary
### Columns 
- date - The date when study seesion took place 
- start_time - The time when I started 
- subject - The subject that i studied
- duration_minutes - The total duration of studies session
- distraction_number - The number of times I got distracted during the studies
- location - Place where i studied
